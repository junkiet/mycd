## 5. Apple requirements relevant to this handoff

Apple’s guidance requires privacy disclosures covering collection, use, sharing and retention/deletion, and an in-app deletion option for apps supporting account creation. Third-party AI sharing of personal data requires disclosure and explicit permission. Community apps need objectionable-content filtering, reporting with timely handling, user blocking and published contact information. These are product requirements as well as policy considerations; this document does not establish that the current build meets them. See [App Review Guidelines, sections 1.2, 5.1.1 and 5.1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).

This handoff concerns the two policy pages and their matching copies. It does not confirm that all other App Store submission items are complete.
